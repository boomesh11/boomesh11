## Executive Summary

Hardware-to-software engineer focused on **bridging real-time embedded systems, sensor pipelines, and edge artificial intelligence**. Background spans designing underwater acoustic and sonar processing pipelines to developing firmware, motor actuation, and telemetry for autonomous ground rovers and space weather platforms.

- **National Distinction:** **1st Place & Team Lead — National Agentic Hackathon 2025** for **SONAR INTEL**, a bio-acoustic early warning IoT system classifying underwater disaster audio with 87.6% accuracy on Raspberry Pi 4 (TinyML/TFLite) with ESP32 hardware actuation.
- **Industry Experience:** Former **Embedded Systems Intern at IQ Math**, developing firmware, real-time telemetry, and hardware-in-the-loop control for the **SARA** autonomous agentic rover.
- **Education:** 3rd Year B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering (Batch 2024–2028) at **St. Joseph's College of Engineering (Anna University)**, Chennai.
- **Core Competencies:** Embedded C/C++, Microcontroller Firmware (ESP32, STM32, ARM Cortex, Raspberry Pi 4), RTOS/Linux, TinyML, Sensor Fusion, PCB Prototyping, and Robotics Actuation.
